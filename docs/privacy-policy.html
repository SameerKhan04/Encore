ENCORE

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 11 October 2026
Package: com.encore.party

ENCORE (“the App”) is a social singing game. This policy explains what information we process when you use the App, including Party Mode and Online Match.

1. Who we are

ENCORE is developed and published by the ENCORE project maintainers. For privacy questions, open an issue on github.com/SameerKhan04/Encore.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you play, the App may process:

  • Microphone audio — short recordings of your singing so the App can turn performance into text (speech-to-text).
  • Transcripts / lyric text — text generated from your recording, plus any edits you make before confirming.
  • Online match data — invite codes, player IDs, scores, turn state, challenge phrases, and related match snapshots when you use Online Match.
  • Peer voice — live microphone audio sent peer-to-peer during Online Match so you can talk and hear the other singer. Voice signaling metadata (connection offers) may pass through our realtime backend; the media itself is exchanged between devices.
  • Basic device / app diagnostics — information needed to run and improve the App (for example crash or connectivity issues via platform services).

We do not ask you to create a traditional username/password account in the current version. Online Match uses generated player identifiers for the session/room.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game loop (challenges, turns, scoring).
  • To transcribe singing into editable lyrics and optionally soft-check whether text looks like song lyrics.
  • To sync Online Match rooms between devices.
  • To carry live voice between Online Match opponents.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

4. Service providers

We use third-party services to operate ENCORE:

  • Google Gemini / Google AI — audio transcription and soft lyric checks. Audio/text you submit for those features is processed by Google under their terms and policies.
  • Supabase — Online Match room storage and realtime sync.
  • Expo / EAS and Google Play — building, distributing, and updating the Android app.

These providers process data on our behalf to provide the App. Their own privacy policies also apply to their services.

5. Data retention

Local Party Mode data stays on your device unless you clear app data. Online Match records may remain on our backend while a match is active and for a limited time afterward for reliability and abuse prevention. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Children

ENCORE is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

7. Your choices

  • You can deny microphone permission; live recording features will not work without it (demo/mock modes may still be available).
  • You can leave an Online Match or stop using online features at any time.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove local data from your device.
  • To request deletion of online match data associated with you, contact us via the GitHub issues link above with your approximate match time and invite code if you have it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect match data in transit (HTTPS) and limit access to backend credentials. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International processing

Our providers may process data in data centers outside your country (including the United States). By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact
